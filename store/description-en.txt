Kick-It! is a 3D football puzzle game about the perfect kick. Drag the ball back, aim, let go, and stop it inside the circle. It sounds simple, but with a limited number of kicks and a ticking clock, every shot counts.

HOW TO PLAY
• Grab the ball and pull back to aim. The further you pull, the harder you kick.
• Let go to kick, and stop the ball inside the target circle to clear the floor.
• Watch your kicks and your time: run out of either, or roll off the edge, and you try the floor again.
• Pinch to zoom, drag to look around, or tap the focus button to frame the ball and the circle.

50 FLOATING FLOORS
Every floor is a little football pitch floating high above a giant stadium, and every 5 floors bring something new:
• Ramps, ledges and fences for bank shots
• Narrow plank bridges
• Trampolines that launch the ball
• Magnets that pull and push it
• Slippery ice and slow sand
• Boost pads that speed it up
• Pinball bumpers
• Portals
• Conveyor belts
• And on the last floors, everything at once

3 STARS ON EVERY FLOOR
Clear a floor to earn your first star. Beat its time target for the second, and use no more than its kick target for the third. Both targets count down under the timer while you play, your best stars show next to each floor, and your total shows in the lobby. Can you collect all 150 stars?

SPEEDRUN MODE
All 50 floors in one run, against the clock. One mistake sends you back to floor 1. Your best time is saved, so you can keep beating it.

16 BALLS TO UNLOCK
Reach new floors, clear floors, land one-kick clears and finish fast speedruns to unlock new balls in the shop: a classic football, a tennis ball, a basketball, a volleyball, a baseball, a beach ball, an 8-ball, a cricket ball, a bowling ball, a golf ball, a disco ball, a watermelon, the Earth, the Moon, a galaxy and a lava ball. Every ball has its own kick effect and its own trail, from fire embers to stardust.

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• Bright cartoon 3D graphics
• Physics that feel right: bank shots off fences, bounces, rolls and slides
• Sound effects and light vibration feedback
• A quick restart button to try a floor again

QUICK TO PLAY, HARD TO MASTER
Each floor takes less than a minute and you only need one finger, so it fits into any break. Getting three stars on every floor is another story.

PLAY ANYWHERE
• Plays fully offline, no internet needed
• No ads
• No account and no data collected
• Small download
