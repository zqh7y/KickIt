# Kick-It! Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Kick-It! is a mobile game made by zzqxck. This policy explains what happens to your data
when you play it.

## In short

Kick-It! does not collect any personal data, does not send any data over the internet, and
does not share any data with anyone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no
tracking. The game works fully offline.

## What the game keeps on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a few things in its own storage on your phone:

- the floors you have reached and the floor you were on
- your best times and the stars you have earned
- the ball you picked in the shop, and the counts its goals use (floors cleared, one-kick clears)

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to the developer or anyone else.
Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes it.

## What the game does not do

- It does not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or any
  other personal information.
- It does not use advertising IDs or other device identifiers.
- It contains no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools.
- It does not sell or share any data, because it collects none.

## Permissions

- **Vibration**: the phone vibrates briefly on kicks and other moments in the game.
- **Internet**: Android lists this permission because the game is built on a web engine
  (Capacitor). The game itself does not connect to the internet.

## Children

Kick-It! is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone,
it collects none from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
