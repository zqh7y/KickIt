# Kick-It! Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This privacy policy applies to Kick-It!, a mobile game made by zzqxck (the developer). It
explains what happens to your data when you play.

## In short

Kick-It! does not collect any personal data and does not share any data with anyone. There
are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game itself never sends anything
over the internet and works fully offline.

## What the game saves on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a few things in its own private storage on your
phone, which other apps cannot read:

- the floors you have reached and the floor you were on
- your best times and the stars you have earned
- the ball you picked in the shop, and the counts its goals use (floors cleared, one-kick clears)

The developer never receives this information.

## Backups

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the game's saved progress
in your own device backup (for example, in your Google account), so it can come back when you
reinstall the game or move to a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account and is
handled by Android and Google; the developer cannot see it.

## How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Your progress stays on your phone until you delete it. To delete it, uninstall the game or clear
its data in your phone's settings (Settings > Apps > Kick-It! > Storage > Clear data). If your
phone backs up apps, you can also remove the game's backup in your Google account's backup
settings.

## What the game does not do

- It does not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or any
  other personal information.
- It does not use advertising IDs or other device identifiers.
- It contains no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools.
- It does not sell or share any data, because it collects none.

## Security

The game collects no data, so it transmits none and the developer stores none. Your progress
stays in the app's private storage on your phone.

## Permissions

- **Vibration**: the phone vibrates briefly on kicks and other moments in the game.
- **Internet**: the app includes Android's internet permission because the engine it is built on
  (Capacitor) adds it by default. The game itself never uses it.

## Links

The lobby has a "Privacy policy" link that opens this page in your web browser. This page is
hosted on GitHub, so GitHub's own privacy statement applies while you view it.

## Children

Kick-It! is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone,
it collects none from children.
